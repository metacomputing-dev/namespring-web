## Why P36-A2 hypothesis does not hold

The Phase 37 plan-doc (`PLAN_PHASE37_NON_SATURATED_FOCUS.md`, §P37-A3)
proposed the hypothesis: "P36-A3 saturation 후 firing-eligible 5p가
0이지만 P36-A2의 4p→5p promotion이 새 firing 5p 생성 가능".

This hypothesis assumes cross-tier interference. Empirically and
structurally it does not hold:

1. **P36-A2 was standard tier, not expert.** Per its commit message
   body at `6b9f73b8`: "lift 10 ct=1 standard fragments 4p -> 5p
   across 7 categories and 5 periods". All 10 lifted fragmentIds are
   in `standard.fragments.json`, not `expert.fragments.json`. The
   expert source pool was untouched by P36-A2.
2. **Expert and standard tiers are selected independently.** The
   tiered matrix selects one fragment per (tier × cell) pair from
   that tier's `<tier>.fragments.json` file. Standard fragment
   selection ordering shifts cannot affect expert fragment selection
   ordering.
3. **Sample regen verifies independence.** The post-P36 merge sample
   regen at `d911ad6d` propagated all 4 sibling deltas
   (P36-A1/A2/A3/A4) into the 32-fixture sample matrix. Re-running
   the firing-pool count on this regenerated state gives the same
   `0/27` result as P36-A3's pre-merge `0/37` measurement (the
   pool is smaller because P36-A3's 10 lifts are now excluded;
   the firing-count structure is identical).

The single mechanism that can create new firing expert 5p candidates
is **fixture/gating surface expansion** (P36-A6 §B option (b)),
which is above-scope for sibling-data phases per the Phase 36 close
recommendations.
