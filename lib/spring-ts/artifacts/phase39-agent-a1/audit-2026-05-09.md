## Why "내일 첫 발걸음을" (vs the audit's other suggestions)

P38-A6 §"Snapshot leak +1 (P38-A2 academic standard 5p)" itemized
three follow-up paths:
- (a) data-fix: "내일 첫 걸음을" → "내일 첫걸음을" (no-space) **or rephrase**
- (b) tooling: extend the gate's regex `(?<![발첫한잰빠])(?<!첫\s)걸음을`
- (c) introduce a snapshot-leak-zero gate as a P39 candidate

The Phase 39 plan-doc commit (`ea1a24e5`) selected (a) and pre-named
two valid forms in the task spec: **"내일 첫 발걸음을"** or
**"한 발자국씩"**. P39-A1 chose **"내일 첫 발걸음을"** for two reasons:

1. **Minimal diff (1 character inserted)**. The other valid form
   "한 발자국씩" forces broader sentence restructuring (어디서
   시작할지 clause has to retarget 발자국 not 첫 X). One-character
   inserts are the safest possible reword discipline at the
   data-fix layer — preserves all surrounding prose verbatim, so
   distinct-hook surface, paragraph count, voice-kind, livingTips,
   gating axes are all guaranteed identical to P38-A2 authoring.
2. **Snapshot-detector regex semantics confirmed**. Pattern
   `(?<![발첫한잰빠])걸음을` flags the standalone form. With the
   space "첫 걸음을" the immediate-preceding char is the space, NOT
   `첫`; lookbehind misses. With "첫 발걸음을" the immediate-preceding
   char to `걸음을` becomes `발`, which IS whitelisted; lookbehind
   exempts. Pattern source: P20-A5 / P21-A5 / P22-A5 / P23-A4
   `measure_p*.mjs` (4 phases byte-stable on this regex). Verified
   here against `artifacts/phase23-agent-a4/measure_p23.mjs` line 98.

The "no-space 첫걸음을" form (audit path (a) variant 1) was
**not** picked — the spec explicitly omitted it and the spaced
"첫 발걸음을" reads more natural in continuous prose.

Paths (b) and (c) remain Phase 39+ candidates if the snapshot-leak
recurrence pattern across multiple phases warrants tooling
investment — P39-A1 closes the data-fix variant only.
