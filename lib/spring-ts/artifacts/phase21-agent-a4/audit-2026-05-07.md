## 2. Path A simulation (lift `MINOR_LIMITED_CATEGORIES`)

**Critical refinement of P18-A3 framing.** `passesMinorGuard`
(`fragment-selector.ts:57`) excludes fragments without explicit
`gating.ageBand` from the candidate pool when the reader is a minor.
So lifting the early-return *only* helps cells where a minor-gated
standard fragment actually exists with paragraphs ≥ 3.

`simulate_path_a.mjs` measures this against the real `_coverage/` data:

| Locked-cat outcome (per cell, 75 total) | count |
|---|---|
| Minor-gated fragment found, paragraphs ≥ 3 | 52 |
| No minor-gated fragment for fixture's ageBand → 1-paragraph fallback | 23 |

The 52 cells that close: all `romance` × 5 periods × 5 fixtures = 25;
all `study_document` × 5 periods × 5 fixtures = 25; `wealth.life.10-19`
(`fx04`) × 1 period × 1 fixture = 1; `wealth.thisWeek.10-19` × 1 = 1.

The 23 cells that still emit a 1-paragraph fallback (all `wealth`):

```
wealth | life       | 0-9   | 4 cells   (no 0-9-gated fragment exists)
wealth | today      | 0-9   | 4 cells   (no minor-gated standard at all)
wealth | today      | 10-19 | 1 cell
wealth | thisWeek   | 0-9   | 4 cells   (only 10-19/20-29 gated)
wealth | thisMonth  | 0-9   | 4 cells   (no minor-gated standard at all)
wealth | thisMonth  | 10-19 | 1 cell
wealth | thisYear   | 0-9   | 4 cells   (no minor-gated standard at all)
wealth | thisYear   | 10-19 | 1 cell
```

**Path A alone result:** 107 → 23 (locked residue) + 32 (fallback) = **55 sub-3**.

Voice/age-appropriateness: existing minor-gated wealth/romance/study_document
fragments use age-fitting prose (e.g. wealth.life.standard.10_19.010 talks
about 용돈/recording habits, not investments). Lifting the early-return is
safe in tone — but `ci:samples-stale` will flip 52 cells, requiring
sample regen + secondary CI gate runs.

## 5. Path C (relax ≤50 target)

If ≤50 is dropped or replaced with a per-axis floor (e.g. "≥3 paragraphs
for ageBand 20-29 and above; ≥1 well-formed paragraph for minors"),
the 107-cell residue is acceptable as a deliberate downshift for
underage readers. No code/data changes needed; only the ratchet metric
in CI/release-checklist would be revised.

This matches the existing minor-floor design philosophy (the
`MINOR_STANDARD_LIMITED_PARAGRAPH` literal was authored deliberately as
a "안전한 안내" downshift — reasonable people can read the 1-paragraph
fallback as a *feature*, not a defect).

## 7. 권고 — Phase 22+ P22-A1 input

**Recommend Path A + B-extended (full closure to 0 sub-3).**

Sub-paths in priority order:
1. **P22-A1 (src)**: lift `MINOR_LIMITED_CATEGORIES` early-return.
   ~5 LOC src diff. Drives 107 → 55 immediately.
   *Voice risk: low — minor-gated fragments are already age-appropriate
   per inspection (used to gate-test on guard, not surface gating).*
2. **P22-A2 (data, fallback)**: author 14 minor-ageband fragments for
   career/health/health_stress/overall as listed in §3. Drives 55 → 23.
   ~420 LOC data. Voice risk identical to P18-A3 Phase 1 (child voice
   density, plain-term gating).
3. **P22-A3 (data, locked-wealth gap)**: author 5 wealth fragments
   (today, thisWeek 0-9, thisMonth, thisYear, life 0-9) as listed in §4.
   Drives 23 → 0. ~150 LOC.

**Rationale for not recommending Path B alone**: 75 residue floor far
exceeds ≤50 (matches P18-A3). **Rationale for not recommending Path C**:
the 1-paragraph minor-fallback prose is generic (`아직 결과를 단정하기보다
작은 습관을 살피는 게 좋아요…`) and identical across all 23 wealth cells
of a minor fixture — relabelled as feature it remains a copy-paste
artifact users will notice (shows up 23×5=115 times in a single report
for a child). Higher quality bar argues for closure not redefinition.

**Rationale for splitting P22-A1 / A2 / A3**: each phase is independently
verifiable. Each commit ≤300 LOC (per memory `feedback_change_policy.md`).
P22-A1 alone is the highest-yield, lowest-LOC step (52 cells/5 LOC).

**Cross-verify hook**: P22-A1 src diff is ~9 lines but flips 52 sample
cells — `ci:samples-stale` will fire across all 5 minor fixtures, and
all secondary narrative gates (`ci:narrative-density`,
`ci:narrative-voice`, `ci:narrative-tag-label-alignment`,
`ci:post-processor-grammar`, `ci:narrative-truncated-endings`) must
pass on regenerated samples.

**Brief/expert tier side effects (P22-A1 implementation note)**: lifting
`MINOR_LIMITED_CATEGORIES` removes `buildMinorFallbackCell` from the
line-332 early-return path. After the lift, brief/standard/expert each
go through `selectFragment` independently with `passesMinorGuard`
filtering. For locked cats × minor fixtures where `standardFrag` exists
but no minor-gated `briefFrag` exists (e.g. some `wealth.{period}.brief`
slots have 0 minor-gated fragments per the inventory in §6 of this
file's pre-commit measurement), `hasAnyFragment=true` and `brief`
becomes `PLACEHOLDER_BRIEF` ('준비 중인 흐름이에요.') — a *regression*
versus the current `buildMinorBriefFallback`-derived 1-liner. P22-A1
implementation MUST pre-measure brief/expert tier deltas (e.g. by
extending `simulate_path_a.mjs` to brief and expert axes) and either
(a) author missing minor-gated briefs/experts before flipping the src
diff, or (b) keep a narrowed minor-brief fallback in the no-`briefFrag`
branch.
