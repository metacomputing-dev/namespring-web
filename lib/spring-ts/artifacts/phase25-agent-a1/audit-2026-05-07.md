## Per-paragraph lexical analysis

### Paragraph #1 — `wealth.thisMonth.standard.child_teen.001` p#4

Source text (data file `data/narrative/wealth/thisMonth/standard.fragments.json`,
fragmentId `wealth.thisMonth.standard.child_teen.001`, paragraph index 4
within `\n\n`-split text):

> 한 달의 작은 결산은 큰 자산이 돼요. 마지막 날 어디에 썼는지
> 한 줄로 정리하면, 다음 달의 결정이 한결 가벼워져요.

Rendered text (5 fixtures, identical post-processor output):

> 한 달의 작은 결산은 큰 자산이 돼요. 마지막 날 어디에 썼는지
> 한 줄로 정리하면, 다음 달의 결정이 한결 가벼워져요.

`결`-bearing tokens, in order: 결산은 / 결정이 / 한결.

| Token | Lexeme | Meaning | POS |
|-------|--------|---------|-----|
| 결산은 | 결산 | financial settlement / month-end accounting close | noun + topic marker `은` |
| 결정이 | 결정 | decision | noun + subject marker `이` |
| 한결 | 한결 | even more so / steadily | bound adverb (한결 + adjective) |

Three distinct lexemes with no shared meaning beyond the etymological
`결` morpheme. `결산` is a sino-Korean compound noun denoting
accounting close (settlement), `결정` denotes a chosen course of
action, and `한결` is the native-Korean intensifier "even more so".
None of the three is a repetition of another. Per task policy:
**HOLD** (genuine lexical diversity).

`결정` count check: `결정` appears exactly once in this paragraph. No
double-사용 of `결정` even in different senses, so no further review.

Paraphrase rejection rationale: Substituting any of the three would
either drop a Korean lexeme that carries domain-specific meaning
(`결산` is the month-end accounting noun the surrounding sentence is
about) or force an awkward alternate (`다음 달의 선택이 한결
가벼워져요` would weaken the decision-confidence frame). The task
policy says to HOLD when 3 distinct lexical items combine, and these
three combine.

### Paragraph #2 — `career.today.standard.age30plus.007` p#1

Source text (data file `data/narrative/career/today/standard.fragments.json`,
fragmentId `career.today.standard.age30plus.007`, paragraph index 1

> 후배·동료의 작은 질문에 짧게라도 답을 주면 자기 자리의
> 방향이 한결 단단해져요. 회의·결재로 마음이 분산될 수
> 있으니, 오전 한 시간만이라도 자기 결정의 시간으로 잡아
> 두면 좋아요.

Rendered text (4 fixtures, post-processor renames `자기 자리의` →
`자기 역할의`):

> 후배·동료의 작은 질문에 짧게라도 답을 주면 자기 역할의
> 방향이 한결 단단해져요. 회의·결재로 마음이 분산될 수
> 있으니, 오전 한 시간만이라도 자기 결정의 시간으로 잡아
> 두면 좋아요.

`결`-bearing tokens, in order: 한결 / 결재로 / 결정의.

| Token | Lexeme | Meaning | POS |
|-------|--------|---------|-----|
| 한결 | 한결 | even more so / steadily | bound adverb |
| 결재로 | 결재 | sign-off / managerial approval | noun + instrumental marker `로` |
| 결정의 | 결정 | decision | noun + genitive marker `의` |

Three distinct lexemes. `한결` is the native-Korean intensifier;
`결재` is the workplace-register noun for managerial approval / sign-
off (formally distinct from `결정`); `결정` is the general decision
noun. Per task policy: **HOLD**.

`결정` count check: `결정` appears exactly once. No further review.

Paraphrase rejection rationale: `결재` carries office-formal register
that `결정` and `서명` do not — replacing it with `서명` (signature)
would lose the workflow nuance ("manager-level approval that may
distract you"); replacing with `결정` would collapse two distinct
lexemes into one and *create* the same-lexical-item repetition the
metric is designed to flag. The current vocabulary is the most
informative.

## Conclusion

The `gyeolCluster.totalCells = 9` measurement after P24-A3 is composed
entirely of paragraphs whose `결` density comes from genuine lexical
diversity, not from same-lexical-item repetition. P24-A3's hold
verdict was correct. P25-A1 confirms the hold with cell-by-cell
lexical analysis (3 distinct `결`-bearing lexemes per paragraph in
both source paragraphs) and adds the per-token POS / register table
that P24-A3 summarized at the cluster level.

A future ratchet wishing to gate `gyeolCluster` should follow the
options P24-A3 surfaced: either (a) raise the threshold to ≥ 4
(currently safe, since both held paragraphs sit at exactly 3), or (b)
refactor the detector to count distinct `결`-bearing lexemes rather
than raw `결` characters — neither option requires further fragment
paraphrase.
