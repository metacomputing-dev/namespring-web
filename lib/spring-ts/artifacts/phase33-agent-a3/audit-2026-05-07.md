## Selection methodology

1. Walked all `data/narrative/<cat>/<period>/expert.fragments.json`
   files (55 files, 558 fragments total) and built a paragraph
   histogram by `\n\n`-splitting each fragment's concatenated text.
   Pre-state: 4p:435 / 5p:77 / 6p:43 / 7p:2 / 8p:1.
2. **Excluded** P28-A2 (10 4p→5p), P30-A4 (10 4p→5p), and P32-A3
   (10 5p→6p) — 30 fragments total — per task constraint
   "P28-A2/P30-A4/P32-A3 lift fragments 무수정". Eligible 5p pool
   after exclusion: 57 fragments.
3. For each eligible 5-paragraph fragment, counted fire frequency
   across 32 `sample-outputs-2026-05-05-phase3/*-tiered.json` tiered
   fixtures (where `selectedFragments.expert.fragmentId === <fragment>`).
4. **Asymmetry vs P32-A3 — explicitly noted.** P32-A3 had a fatter
   positive-fire pool (10 lifts × ~1 fire each). After exclusion,
   the eligible pool here only contains **6 positive-fire frags**
   (6+4+4+2+2+1 = 19 cumulative), and 4 of those are romance category.
   Therefore the methodology shifted from "10 lifts of ~1 fire each" to
   "2 high-fire lifts (10 fires combined) + 8 source-coverage fillers
   (0 fires) across health/movement/wealth/study_document". Both
   approaches satisfy "~10 lift" and "+~9 rendered" but the
   distribution is different. Helper script:
   `artifacts/phase33-agent-a3/find_5p_candidates.mjs`
   (output: `candidates-2026-05-08.json`).
5. **Heavy health/health_stress concentration (6 of 10) is structural,
   not a design choice.** The eligible pool is 22 health + 4
   health_stress out of 57 (46% of the post-exclusion universe).
