## Summary

cells" with the breakdown "84 wildcard-floor 2-paragraph cells + 107
single-paragraph `overall.life.standard.age_*` cells".

**The breakdown is inverted.** Re-measurement (see
`sub3-snapshot.json` and `analyze-no-fid.json`) gives:

- 84 cells from data fragments (axis + ageband fragments living in
  `_coverage/`) — all 2-paragraph; reachable from owned scope.
- 107 cells with **no fragmentId** at all. Source is the literal
  `MINOR_STANDARD_LIMITED_PARAGRAPH` in
  `src/report/tiered/build-tiered-matrix.ts:73-81`, emitted via
  `buildMinorFallbackCell` either as an early-return override
  (line 332-335 for wealth / romance / study_document at minor age
  band) or as a no-fragment fallback (line 354).

These are NOT `overall.life.standard.age_*` cells; they are
`{wealth,romance,study_document,career,health,health_stress,overall}.{
period}` byCategory rows for minor age band fixtures.

## Phase 2 — deferred

Phase 2 (author 32 minor-ageband fragments) is out of scope for A3:

- Even with Phase 2, the floor stays at 75 from `MINOR_LIMITED_CATEGORIES`,
  far above the ≤50 target.
- New-fragment authoring is a different risk profile from mechanical
  splitting — voice/tag/density gate exposure multiplies.
- Phase 1 already caught one 세운 plain-term collision in a P3 draft
  (rewritten before commit) — the risk is real.

Recommend a follow-up Phase to decide between:

- (a) Lift `MINOR_LIMITED_CATEGORIES` early-return in src/. Allows
  authored fragments to override the 75 wealth/romance/study_document
  cells. Reaches ≤50 target.
- (b) Author 32 minor-ageband `_coverage/` fragments. Drops sub-3 to
  75 but does NOT reach ≤50.
- (c) Relax target to ≤80 or remove the metric.
