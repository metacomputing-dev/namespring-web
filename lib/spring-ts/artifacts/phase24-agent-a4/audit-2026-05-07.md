## Root cause

Two-layer interaction surfaces a real prose-quality issue at the
overlap:

1. **P23-A2 authored** `romance.life.standard.minor.teen.001` (and the
   parallel `today/thisWeek/thisMonth/thisYear` siblings) on 2026-05-07
   with `gating.agePhase: ["early_teen", "late_teen"]` and
   `gating.ageBand: ["10-19"]` to close P22-A6 R1 sub-3 residue. The
   teen-band para1 used `결혼이나 큰 약속 같은 단정은 한참 뒤의
   이야기로 두고…` — the wording explicitly defers marriage talk to "much
   later", inheriting the same negation framing already used by the
   pre-existing `romance.life.standard.teen.001` (line 100) and
   `romance.thisYear.standard.teen.001`.

2. **P12-A5's audience_leak detector** lists `결혼` in the 27-term adult
   vocabulary list and flags any bare-word occurrence in fixtures
   whose audit-script audience tier is `infant` / `child` / `preteen`.
   The audit script bins ages with `preteen` ≤ 13 (`audit-phase12.mjs`
   `audienceTier()` at line 33), while the actual fragment selector at
   `feature-selector.ts:202-204` bins `child_0_9` for age < 10 and
   `early_teen` for age 10-14. The 13-year-old fixture (#04) sits in
   the boundary cell where these two age models disagree.

P12-A5 §B5 already documented this overlap as informational ("0 hits at
the data layer; left as informational"). P23-A2's authoring lifted that
0 to 1, with the affected fragment being one P23-A2 newly authored.

## Why fix rather than carve out the detector

Three reasons:

1. **Existing doctrine is explicit.** `_modifier_age/phrases.json:73`
   (teen subtone) already prescribes `직업·결혼은 단정하지 않고
   '관심·탐색'으로. 압박 어휘 회피.` — bare `결혼` token is exactly the
   pressure vocabulary the doctrine says to avoid. P23-A2's
   humanInterpretation states "no marriage determinism", but the bare
   word still leaks the concept into the visible text.
2. **Fix is surgical.** Drop the two-token span `결혼이나 ` from the
   compound noun phrase `결혼이나 큰 약속 같은 단정`. Result reads
   `큰 약속 같은 단정은 한참 뒤의 이야기로 두고…` — natural Korean,
   identical sentence shape, same deferral semantics.
3. **Carve-out is more expensive.** Documenting a permanent exception
   for the 13-year early_teen boundary cell would add detector
   complexity for one of two paths: either bump the audit's `preteen`
   bound from ≤ 13 to ≤ 9 (matching `child_0_9` only) and lose one
   safety bucket for ages 10-13, or add a per-fragment whitelist mechanism
   for "negation-style" adult-vocab usage. Either widens the surface
   for future leaks. Dropping one word does not.
