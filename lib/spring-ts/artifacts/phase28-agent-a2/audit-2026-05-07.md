## Selection methodology

1. Walked all `data/narrative/<cat>/<period>/expert.fragments.json` files
   (55 files) and built a paragraph histogram by `\n\n`-splitting each
   fragment's concatenated text tokens.
2. For each 4-paragraph fragment, walked the 32 `sample-outputs-2026-05-05-phase3/*.json`
   tiered fixtures and counted how many fixtures referenced the fragmentId
   (so the 5th paragraph would actually surface in real outputs, not be
   author-only depth).
3. Ranked 4p fragments by fire count and selected 10 across diverse
   (category, period) pairs — 9 distinct (cat, period) pairs.

## Theme of 5th paragraph

For each fragment, the 5th paragraph adds one of:
- **Long-horizon insight**: how this period's pattern compounds into the
  next (e.g. `overall.thisYear.expert.strong.neutral.005`'s `희신` quarterly
  signal; `wealth.thisYear.expert.weak.neutral.004`'s `bigyeon` trust over
  分期).
- **Lifelong root note**: how this stage's roots persist (e.g.
  `family.life.expert.young_caregiver.008`'s `부모궁` → 평생 자기 응원;
  `health.life.expert.child.001`'s `식신` 결 over lifetime).
- **Cohort-specific interleave**: how cohort gating modifies tempo (e.g.
  `health_stress.today.expert.male_30_39.001`'s 30-50대 한계
  의식; `romance.thisMonth.expert.adult.weak.001`'s 약한 일간
  천천히 익는 자리).
- **Multi-tag synthesis**: how 격국용신/통관용신/병약용신/조후 mesh
  with 길신 (천덕/월덕) for richer expert-tier reading.
