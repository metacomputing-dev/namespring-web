## 6. Recommendations for Phase 39+

1. **Forbidden set tightening**: Phase 39 should treat all fixtures at 17
   AND all fixtures at 16 (16 fixtures total) as forbidden-to-lift. The
   set is now `{05,12,13,32,33}` at 17 and `{09,10,15,17,21,23,24,25,26,28,29}`
   at 16. To get distinct hooks 130 → 140 (+10), the safe target headroom
   has shrunk to ~13 fixtures. Each fragment should target ≥2 safe fixtures
   where possible (chosen via shared-tuple targeting).

2. **Strength-dim leverage**: P38-A3 used 4-dim `g × ab × season × strength`
   for 7 of 10 fragments to cleanly exclude STRONG fixtures from WEAK targets
   (and vice versa). The next phase should consider adding `yongshinAlignment`
   as a 5th dim where 4-dim is insufficient (e.g., `aligned` vs `neutral` vs
   `conflicting` may discriminate further between same-strength siblings).

3. **Consensus-aware fixtures**: #15 and #21 share the same engine-truth
   tuple (`M/40-54/spring/WEAK`) — both currently at 16. Their fortune
   curves diverge on `multi_axis` and `consensus_aware` evaluator modes.
   A `evaluatorMode`-gated fragment could target one without the other,
   if the fragment-selector exposes that key.

4. **gyeokguk-second-dim gates**: P36-A4 #8 used `gyeokguk:["jeonggwangyeok"]`
   for the first time. P38-A3 did not author a gyeokguk-gated fragment
   (because the only `jeonggwangyeok` fixtures `#5` and `#25` are now
   forbidden). Future phases should consider `sikshingyeok` (fired by #19,
   #34, both safe), `siksinyeok`, or other gyeokguk values that surface in
   ≥1 safe fixture.
