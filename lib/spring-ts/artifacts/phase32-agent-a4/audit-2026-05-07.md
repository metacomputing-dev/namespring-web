## 3. Authoring decisions

### Effective-fixture-feature targeting

The fragment selector matches against the **runtime FeatureVector**, not the request input. Two fixtures (#14, #35) carry `surfacePalace+surfaceNaeum=true`, which in this codebase causes `analyzeSaju` to return `emptySaju()`, which makes `feature.ageBand=70+` and `feature.birthSeason=winter` regardless of request birth.year. This was discovered after fragment 7 (academic/thisYear) and fragment 10 (overall/today), originally gated for `female × 30-39 × summer` to fire on #35, ended up dormant. Both were redirected:

- 7 → `female × 70+ × autumn` (fires on #17)
