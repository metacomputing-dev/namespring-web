## Methodology

1. **Diagnosis (P27-A6 snapshot)**: P27-A6 audit identified
   `28-jonggyeok-jonggang-tiered` as the sole 18-hook fixture, +2 above
   the next tier (05-park-minji, 13-oh at 17). The +2 lift was
   attributable to two P27-A1 fragments authored with 3-dim gating
   `(gender=male, ageBand=70+, birthSeason=autumn)`:
     - `romance.today.brief.male.70plus.autumn.012`
       (hook: `오랜 인연에게 차 한 잔 권해요.`)
     - `academic.thisMonth.brief.male.70plus.autumn.010`
       (hook: `한 분야를 깊이 들여다봐요.`)
2. **Fixture demographic enumeration**: walked all 32 sample fixtures'
   `request.birth` and applied `feature-selector.ts:toSeason()`
   (lichun-aligned: months 2-4 spring, 5-7 summer, 8-10 autumn, 11-1
   winter). Two fixtures match `male × 70+ × autumn`:
     - `25-jonggyeok-jonggwan-tiered` (year=1933, month=9, gender=male)
     - `28-jonggyeok-jonggang-tiered` (year=1938, month=8, gender=male)
3. **Discriminator selection**: extracted per-fixture
   `dayMasterStrength` from each fixture's emitted
   `cell.selectedFragments.brief.gating.dayMasterStrength`. The two
   fixtures occupy disjoint dayMasterStrength bands:
     - `25-jonggyeok-jonggwan` → `WEAK` / `EXTREME_WEAK` (jonggwan = 종관격, 신약 종격)
     - `28-jonggyeok-jonggang` → `STRONG` / `EXTREME_STRONG` (jonggang = 종강격, 신강 종격)
   This is the cleanest 4th gating dimension that splits the two
   fixtures while keeping the authoring intent (the original P27-A1
   prose "오래 본 사람에게 가벼운 차 한 잔" / "좋아하는 분야 한 권을
   차분히 읽어요" is consonant with weak-pole 종관 — settled, observant,
   inward — rather than strong-pole 종강 which leans assertive).
4. **Edit**: appended `"dayMasterStrength": ["WEAK", "EXTREME_WEAK"]` to

   (`fragment-selector.ts:specificityScore`) counts non-empty gating

   and is excluded on `28` (no match — gating fails the `STRONG` band).
5. **Build + regen**: `npm install` (saju-ts/spring-ts/seed-ts);

## Why ≤17 fallback rather than ≤16 primary

The P28-A4 task brief framed ≤16 as the primary target and ≤17 as the
fallback. After delivering max=17, reaching ≤16 would require touching
either `05-park-minji-late-night-female` or `13-oh-extreme-weak-continuous`
(both held at 17, unchanged from P27-A5). Neither path is clean:

- **`05-park-minji-late-night-female`** carries two narrow-gated P27-A1
  contributors (`health_stress.thisYear.brief.female.thirties.winter.013`
  and `romance.thisWeek.brief.female.thirties.winter.013`). Both are
  authored with 3-dim gating `(gender=female, ageBand=30-39,
  birthSeason=winter)` — the **only** fixture matching this exact
  triple is `05`. Adding a 4th dimension would make either or both
  fragments dormant (regressing P27-A2's `dormantHookStringsByText = 0`
  ratchet).
- **`13-oh-extreme-weak-continuous`** held at 17 *before* P27-A1's
  expansion (was the prior ceiling fixture). Touching its incumbent
  hook fragments would broaden scope beyond P27-A1's owned territory
  and introduce risk of pre-Phase-27 hook regression.

The owned scope of this commit is a 2-fragment gating tighten that:
(a) surgically addresses the +2 outlier on `28-jonggyeok-jonggang` that
P27-A1 introduced, (b) restores the pre-P27-A1 ceiling (17), (c) widens
the gate margin from 2 → 3 (giving Phase 29+ headroom for one more
+12-fragment hook richness round before re-encountering this
threshold), and (d) preserves P27-A1's distinct=60 delivery without
regressing dormant counts.

Hitting ≤16 cleanly would require a different shape of work: either
(i) a P26-A1 sibling-style 3p→4p+ fragment for one of the 17-fixture
hooks paired with new narrow gating on its lowest-priority cell, or
(ii) a `--max-hooks` ratchet decision (ratchet to 22-25) combined with
a Phase 29+ lateral-expansion strategy. Both are out-of-scope for this
single-intent commit.
