## Flow-cluster防 design

P29-A1 root-cause analysis showed `reduceOverusedGyeol` substitutes
`결X` particle forms (`결이`, `결로`, `결의`) to `흐름X` with budget reset
at every tag-token boundary. To keep `flowCluster.totalCells = 0`:

1. **No `결이` after a tag token** in any new 5th paragraph — 결X tokens
   only appear in non-tag-adjacent positions.
2. **Used `기운이`, `호흡`, `자리`, `리듬` for "flow/energy" semantics**
   instead of `결이`/`결로` after tags (P29-A1 lesson applied
   prophylactically).
3. **Each new paragraph's source has ≤1 literal `흐름이`** — plus zero
   `결X` substitution-eligible tokens after tag boundaries — so the
   rendered count stays ≤2 per paragraph (well under the ≥3 cluster
   threshold).
