academic/thisYear, movement/thisMonth/life). Career (5/5 periods) was fully
untouched — strong cluster candidate.

### Concentration discipline (max ≤19 ratchet)

Pre-state max=17 at `05-park-minji-late-night-female` and
`13-oh-extreme-weak-continuous`. Margin to 19 ratchet = 2. Authoring rules:
- Never gate on `(female × 30-39 × winter)` — fires on #5, would lift to 18.
- Never gate on `(female × 40-54 × winter)` — fires on #13, would lift to 18.
- 16-tier fixtures (#9 #10 #17 #28 #32 #33) tolerate at most +1 each (lift
  to 17, ties existing ceiling, no max change).
- Best targets: 7-13 hook fixtures with most headroom.

### #4 vs #8 disambiguation via dayMasterStrength

Two slots target `(female × 30-39 × autumn)`:
- #4 (career/thisYear): female × 30-39 × autumn matches both #8 (10 hooks) and #33 (16 hooks)
- #8 (study_document/life): same triple

Without further dim, both fragments would fire on both fixtures, pushing
#33 to 18 (margin 1, regressing concentration risk). Solution: split via
`dayMasterStrength`:
- #8 (career/thisYear) gates `WEAK | EXTREME_WEAK` → fires on #8 only (dms=WEAK)
- #33 (study_document/life) gates `STRONG | EXTREME_STRONG` → fires on #33 only (dms=STRONG)

### #5 narrowing via agePhase

`career/life` candidate `male × 40-54 × spring` matches #12 (1980, 46, late_40s),
#15 (1986, 40, early_40s), #21 (1986, 40, early_40s). All three fixtures
qualify; all three would lift +1.
- #12 was at 15 → lift to 16 (acceptable but adds another 16-tier fixture)
- #15 / #21 were at 13 → lift to 14 (safe)

Restricting via `agePhase=early_40s` excludes #12, fires only on #15 and #21
(both at 13 → 14). Cleaner concentration profile.

## 7. Recommendations for Phase 34+

1. **Hook concentration ceiling at 17 with 4 fixtures (margin 2 vs ratchet
   19, margin 3 vs gate 20)**. The 17-tier now contains #5, #13, #32, #33.
   Phase 34+ adding another batch of distinct hooks should target fixtures
   currently at 8-14 distinct hooks (e.g., #6 #11 #14 #15 #16 #18 #19 #20
   #21 #22 #25 #27 #34 #35) to keep max ≤19.

2. **Career cluster fully populated**. Career/today/thisWeek/thisMonth/
   thisYear/life now each carry a 3-4 dim hook contributing one more
   distinct hook. Future career hook expansion should target different
   demographic intersections (e.g., female × midlife × spring,
   male × 20-29 × winter) to avoid retreading the same triple.

3. **Template-engine 결→흐름 rewrite is observable in hook strings**.
   `template-engine.ts:91` `작은 결을 차곡차곡 → 작은 흐름을 차곡차곡` and
   `:779` `결을 → 흐름을` general rewrite affect emitted hook strings.
   Authors using `결을` in templates should expect emit-side `흐름을`. The
   text uniqueness check (ci:hook-coverage) operates on emitted strings.

4. **#4 / #8 dayMasterStrength split is reusable**. When two prospective
   fragments target `(gender, ageBand, birthSeason)` triples that match
   multiple fixtures (here #8 and #33 both female × 30-39 × autumn), use
   `dayMasterStrength` (or `dayMasterElement`, `gyeokguk`, `agePhase`) as
   a 4th dim to split. Two narrow fragments emit one each rather than
   stacking double on a single fixture.
