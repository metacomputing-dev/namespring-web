## Pool methodology refinement

P38-A2's audit warned: "P39 5p-lift candidate budget at +10: 1 ct=1
+ 3 ct=2 = 1+6 = 7 cells — short by 3." That projection assumed
40-character first-paragraph prefix matching, which misses cells
where the post-processor has rewritten 결-suffix particles (e.g.,
`이동 결은` -> `이동운은` via the 결->운 surface rewrite, or `결은` ->
`흐름은` via reduceOverusedGyeol).

This phase's `select-candidates.mjs` normalizes both source and
sample first-paragraph prefixes by collapsing 결X / 흐름X / 운X to
a wildcard before keying. After normalization the 4p ct>=1 pool
expanded to **5 ct=1 + 12 ct=2 + 0 ct=3 + 5 ct>=4 (1 wealth ct=4
+ 4 wealth ct=12-13)**, comfortably above the +10 budget. The 5
prior-phase normalizing collisions are all in `romance` (which is
out of P39-A3's lift scope), so the wider net does not introduce
new ambiguity to the candidate set.
