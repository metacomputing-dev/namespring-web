## 3. Mechanism diagnosis

For each minor fixture × period, `passesMinorGuard`
(`fragment-selector.ts:57`) admits only fragments whose `gating.ageBand`
explicitly includes the reader's band. Pre-fix, the candidate pool
for `study_document.{life,today,thisWeek}.standard` minor lookup was:

- `study_document.{period}.standard.young.004` — `gating.ageBand = ["0-9","10-19"]`, **5 paragraphs**.
- `study_document.{period}.standard.ageband.student.501` (in
  `_coverage/min14-study-document-plain-floor.fragments.json`) —
  `gating.ageBand = ["0-9","10-19","20-29"]`, **2 paragraphs**.

Both pass the minor guard with `specificityScore = 1` (one non-wildcard
gating dimension). `selectFragment`
(`fragment-selector.ts:99-120`) picks among ties via FNV-1a hash of
`(seedKey | category | period | depth)`. For these 7 cells the hash
landed on `student.501` (2 paragraphs), tripping the sub-3 floor.

To deterministically beat both rivals, a new fragment must reach
`specificityScore ≥ 2`. Adding `agePhase` alongside `ageBand` lifts
specificity to 2 while preserving minor-only voice scope. Same
shape P23-A2 chose for `romance.{life,today,thisWeek}.standard.minor.*`.
