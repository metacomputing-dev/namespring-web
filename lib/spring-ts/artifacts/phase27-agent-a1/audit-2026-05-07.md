## Methodology

1. P26-A6 audit identified the gap (48 distinct hooks across 32
   fixtures, max=17). Recommendation #1 frames the target as 48 → ~60.
2. Slot inventory: 21 of the 55 `<category>/<period>` brief slots
   carried no `hook` field. P27-A1 selected 12 of these 21 slots,
   prioritizing diverse demographic coverage (youth, midlife winter,
   senior 70+, etc.).
3. Per-fixture feature reconstruction: the 32 fixtures' `birth`
   (gender, year, month) were combined with `src/report/tiered/feature-selector.ts`'s
   `toSeason()` to produce `(gender, ageBand, birthSeason)` triples
   per fixture. Note: `toSeason()` uses lichun-aligned ranges (months
   2-4 = spring, 5-7 = summer, 8-10 = autumn, 11-1 = winter), NOT
   calendar quarters.
4. Per-fixture hook count was extracted from the P26-A6 post-merge
   sample matrix (max=17 at `13-oh-extreme-weak-continuous`,
   `28-jonggyeok-jonggang` = 16 etc.).
5. Distinct hook expansion needs each new fragment to satisfy:
   (a) text uniqueness against the 48 existing hooks,
   (b) selector specificity (count of distinct gating fields)
       strictly greater than the existing winner in the target slot
       for at least one fixture,
   (c) hook ≤24 Korean code points after `normalizeRenderedText`,
   (d) cumulative concentration projection ≤19 (task ceiling, margin
       1 vs the `--max-hooks=20` gate).
6. Per-slot specificity ceiling was measured by enumerating
   `cell.selectedFragments.brief.gating` across the 32 fixtures.
   Slots whose ceiling was 1 dim received 2-dim gating; slots whose
   ceiling was 2 dim received 3-dim gating.
7. Concentration projection per fixture was simulated by counting
   how many planned fragments would fire on each fixture using the
   reconstructed `(gender, ageBand, birthSeason)` triples. None of
   the 12 candidates target `13-oh-extreme-weak-continuous` (the 17-hook
   ceiling fixture); concentration max is projected to land at
   `28-jonggyeok-jonggang` = 16 + 2 = 18 (intentional) by combining
   `male × 70+ × autumn` candidates #7 (romance/today) and #9
   (academic/thisMonth).
8. After authoring, `npm run samples:regen` regenerated the 36
   `*-tiered.json` artifacts (and `index.json`) from the freshly-built
   `saju-ts` dist; `ci:hook-coverage` confirmed 60 / 60 / 0 / 0
   (authored / distinct / dormant slot / dormant text).
9. **Two iterations were required** because the first iteration
   incorrectly mapped 17-kim-senior (`gender=female, age=70+,
   birth.month=8`) to `birthSeason=summer` using calendar-quarter
   logic, when the engine's `toSeason()` returns `autumn` for
   month=8. Two fragments (#4 `health_stress/today` and #10
   `academic/life`) were originally gated on `birthSeason=summer`
   and dormant; both were rewritten to `birthSeason=autumn` and the
   regen confirmed both fired on 17-kim-senior with the planned
   3-dim specificity beating the 2-dim and 1-dim incumbents.
10. `ci:hook-concentration` confirmed max moved 17 → 18 (margin 2 vs
    threshold 20). `ci:samples-stale` PASS (0 violations).
11. `test:namespring-compat` 202 / 0 PASS held.

## Recommendations for Phase 27+

1. **Continue distinct-hook expansion 60 → ~70** would push max
   concentration toward the gate threshold. With max=18 and margin 2,
   one more +12-fragment phase risks crossing 20. Either:
   (a) lift the `--max-hooks` threshold ratchet (P21-A3) to e.g. 22
       and continue narrow-gating expansion, or
   (b) shift authoring strategy to per-fixture lateral expansion
       (multi-render brief variants) rather than additional distinct
       hooks (P26-A6 §Rec #8).

2. **P27-A2 dormant close**: this phase preserves the 1 dormant hook
   text (`career.thisWeek.brief.age40plus.009` "쌓아 온 결이 자리를
   받쳐 줘요.") for the planned P27-A2 work. After P27-A2 lands,
   `dormantHookStringsByText` should reach 0.

3. **Tail emit-count distribution**: 5 of the 12 new fragments emit
   at exactly 1 (`romance.thisWeek.female.thirties.winter.013`,
   `study_document.thisMonth.male.midlife.winter.009`,
   `wealth.thisWeek.female.teen.013`, plus pre-existing tail). They
   are NOT dormant (emit ≥ 1) so `ci:hook-coverage` is satisfied.
   The narrow-gating contract is intentional — these fragments
   target specific demographic intersections without diluting the
   broader-gated fragments' surface coverage.

4. **birthSeason boundary documentation**: the engine's `toSeason()`
   uses lichun-aligned ranges (months 2-4 spring, 5-7 summer, 8-10
   autumn, 11-1 winter), NOT calendar quarters. P27-A1 first
   iteration mistakenly used calendar-quarter mapping and produced
   2 dormant fragments. A future fragment-authoring helper or
   data-contract note could surface this convention to prevent
   recurrence.
