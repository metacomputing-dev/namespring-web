fragments with **0 positive-fire candidates** (all firing 5p slots
taken by 5 prior lift phases)". The mechanism:

1. The 32 fixtures × 55 cell-tier slots = 1760 expert tier cells, of

   periods`, see `ci:brief-tier-placeholder` fixturesSkipped).
2. Of the 1705 expert-tier cells, 1547 / 118 / 40 hit the 4p / 5p / 6p
   render bands respectively. The 6p tier specifically requires the
   selected fragment to have an authored 6th paragraph AND fire on the
   gating tuple of that cell.
3. P28-A2 (11) → P30-A4 (5) → P32-A3 (10) → P33-A3 (10) → P34-A3 (9)
   → P36-A3 (10) = 60 lifted fragments cumulatively, with 40 firing on
   distinct rendered cells (some lift candidates fire on 0 cells, are
   "filler"). Each successive phase had progressively fewer firing
   candidates remaining as the unique-firing-tuple pool exhausts.
4. P36-A3 selection: the 10 picks were 100 % filler (0 fires). The
   eligible firing pool at A3's branch checkpoint was 0 of 37
   post-exclusion candidates. The +10 P6 paragraphs are durable
   narrative-data depth; rendered cell count cannot move without
   expanding the fixture surface (more fixtures or more gating tuples
   per cell).

### Phase 37+ implications (saturation pivot follow-up)

The advisor brief specifically tasked P36-A6 to "P36-A3 의 saturation
pivot 후속 권고 (Phase 37+)". Three actionable strategies:

**Option (a) Pivot to standard 5p band continuation** — the standard
5p source pool has not yet saturated (P36-A2 just hit 300; eligible
post-exclusion pool is still substantial: 130 4p − ~60 already-promoted
= ~70 candidates). Phase 37 could continue 4p→5p lifts (300 → ~310)
without saturation. The standard tier has 1705 cells (vs expert's
identical 1705 cells but with 4-tier render distribution that
concentrates at 4p), giving ample headroom.

**Option (b) Expand the fixture/gating surface** — the rendered 6p
band is bounded by `(firing fragments × cells per firing fragment)`.
Adding new fixtures (or re-running fixture authoring with broader
gating coverage) would unbottleneck the render pipeline. This is a
**meta-pivot from data-author phases to fixture-author phases** and
above the typical sibling-data scope. Candidate Phase 37 sibling
A_meta to expand the 32 → 33+ fixture set.

**Option (c) Defer 6p band expansion until fixture pivot** — accept
that the 6p band saturated at 40 cells with the current 32 fixtures,
treat P36-A3's +10 source authoring as deposited capacity for the next
fixture-expansion phase, and skip expert-tier siblings in Phase 37+
data phases (Phase 33 and Phase 35 both already had this property —
no expert sibling — so this would be a data-only pattern continuation).

**Recommended priority**: (a) > (c) > (b). The (a) standard-5p
continuation is straightforward sibling-data work; (b) is genuinely
above-scope (fixture authoring); (c) is a passive deferral that risks
under-utilising the now-larger 6p source pool (73 fragments, of which
33 = 73 − 40 are unfired — those become firable only when fixtures
expand). Phase 37+ planning should explicitly choose (a) and queue
(b) for a future fixture-author phase.
