## Phase 28+ recommendations

### High priority

1. **Hook concentration ceiling decision** (continued from P27-A5 §Rec
   #2). Post-Phase-27 max=18 with margin 2 vs `--max-hooks=20` gate.
   Phase 28+ adding ~10 more distinct hooks could push max to 19 or 20.
   Decision required: (a) tighten narrow-gating discipline so new
   fragments avoid the current top-7 fixtures (28, 05, 13, 09, 10, 32,
   33), (b) ratchet `--max-hooks` to 22-25 in a Phase 28 commit (gives
   3-5 phases of headroom), or (c) shift to lateral expansion (hook
   variants per cell-rendered text — see Rec #4).

2. **Hook richness continuation** (continued from P25-A5 §Rec #6,
   P26-A6 §Rec #1, P27-A5 §Rec #3). 21 of 55 `<cat>/<period>` brief
   slots originally carried no `hook` field. P27-A1 added 12, leaving
   9 slots still bare. Phase 28 candidate: complete the remaining 9
   slots with continued narrow-gating to avoid concentration-max
   regression. Target: distinct hooks 60 → ~69. Concentration impact
   should be modest if new hooks gate to currently-low-concentration
   fixtures.

3. **Hook richness saturation strategy** (continued from P26-A6 §Rec
   #8, P27-A5 §Rec #3). With 60 distinct hooks at avg 6.1 emits each
   = 366 occurrences vs theoretical ceiling ~32 fixtures × ~5 hook-
   eligible cells × 2 = 320 (back-of-envelope). The corpus is approaching
   saturation. Lateral expansion options:
   - (a) hook variants per cell-rendered text (multi-render brief
     enabling 2-3 hook strings per cell with deterministic selection by
     audience features),
   - (b) tighter audience cohort × dayMasterStrength × seasonContext ×
     yongshinAlignment gating to push specificityScore higher and let
     more authored hooks fire deterministically.

   300 LOC pacing applies. Note that P27-A3's lift moves cells *from*
