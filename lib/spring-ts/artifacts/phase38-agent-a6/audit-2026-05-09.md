## Snapshot leak +1 (P38-A2 academic standard 5p)

**The 1 unpredicted delta from Phase 38**. The post-merge snapshot
captures `postProcessorLeaks.totalOccurrences = 1` under the
`walk_eul` detector pattern (`(?<![발첫한잰빠])걸음을`).

**Source**: `data/narrative/academic/today/standard.fragments.json`
fragment `academic.today.standard.balanced.009` (P38-A2 P5
authoring) contains the substring "내일 첫 걸음을 어디서". The
snapshot detector flags this because the negative lookbehind
exempts only `첫걸음을` (no space), not `첫 걸음을` (with space).

**Production CI gate impact**: **none**. `ci:post-processor-grammar`
docstring (line 25) explicitly states `걸음을` is phonetically
valid; the gate only flags compounds like `결로 → 걸음로`. P38-A2's
authoring passed the gate (commit body confirms PASS) — the gate
**cannot see** what the snapshot detector flags.

**Why this matters**: the snapshot detector is the broader
"diagnostic surface"; the CI gate is the production lock. The +1
regression is real but does not break the 21-gate sweep. P38-A2
authoring discipline followed the gate's contract; the leak is a
**gap in the gate's coverage** vs the snapshot's coverage.

**Phase 39+ follow-up filed** (see §C below): two paths exist —
(a) data-fix in a later phase ("내일 첫 걸음을" → "내일 첫걸음을"
no-space, or rephrase), (b) tooling: extend the gate's regex to
match the 띄어쓰기 form (`(?<![발첫한잰빠])(?<!첫\s)걸음을`), or
(c) introduce a snapshot-leak-zero gate as a P39 candidate.
