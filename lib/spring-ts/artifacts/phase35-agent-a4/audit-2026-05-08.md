## 6. Recommendations for Phase 36+

1. **Hook concentration ceiling at 17 with 4 fixtures (margin 2 vs 19 ratchet,
   margin 3 vs 20 gate)**. The 17-tier still contains #5, #13, #32, #33. The
   16-tier expanded from 8 → 9 fixtures (added #12). Phase 36+ adding distinct
   hooks should target fixtures currently at 8-12 distinct hooks (e.g., #08
   #11 #16 #20 #22 #30 #31 #34 #35) or under-touched 0-9 / 10-19 / 20-29
   demographic intersections to keep max ≤19.

2. **Engine-truth feature vectors should be the basis for all gating decisions**.
   Naive month-based heuristics misclassify #14 and #35 (and possibly others).
   The recommended workflow is:
   - Run `analyzeSajuSafe(birth, options) → buildFeatureVector(saju.summary,
     birth, targetDate)` per fixture before drafting fragment gating
   - Use the engine-derived `gender × ageBand × birthSeason × dayMasterStrength`
     tuple as the canonical demographic
   - Verify each new fragmentId fires post-regen via `samples-stale` snapshot

3. **3-dim and 4-dim gating combined exhausting popular tuples**. After P35-A4,
   most `(gender × ageBand × birthSeason)` and `(gender × ageBand ×
   birthSeason × WEAK)` triples that match a sample fixture are claimed by an
   authored fragment. Phase 36+ may need:
   - 4-dim splits with `gyeokguk` (e.g., `geobjaegyeok`, `bigyeongyeok`) to
     differentiate same demographic but different gyeokguk fixtures
   - 4-dim splits with `agePhase` (finer than `ageBand`) for cohorts that share
     a band but differ in early/late phase
   - Different `cat × period` slots from the still-claimed pool (most slots have
     ≤4 hooks; the 9 untouched gating-triple regions inside claimed slots
     remain feasible).

4. **Hook displacement vs net lift**. Of 10 new fragments, all 10 fired on
   fixture-cell pairs whose target slot had `_coverage`/wildcard/no-hook
   incumbent, so all 13 fixture-cell lifts contributed +1 to per-fixture
   distinct hook count. P34-A4 had 6/10 displacements (net 0 lift on those
   cells). P35-A4 chose target slots more carefully (selecting cells where the
   incumbent fragment had no `hook` field) and achieved 13/13 net lifts.
