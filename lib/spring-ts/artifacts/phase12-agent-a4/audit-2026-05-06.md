# Phase 12 Agent A4 — Fragment reuse high-overlap audit + diversification

Date: 2026-05-06
Owner: P12-A4
Scope: identify and diversify expert-depth fragments with accidental
high-overlap closings (rendered prose appearing in 27-32 fixtures), per
the P11-A5 cross-fixture audit observation that 47/50 cells with
distinct ratio < 0.3 were expert-depth and many shared closing prose
across fragments with different gating.

## Headline

P12-A4 diversified shared closings in **134 fragment edits** across 4
commits, dropping the heatmap's `in_100pct_fixtures` from **5 to 1**
(remaining: the intentional `family.*.expert.wildcard.001` floor template
that fires for every chart by design). The expert-depth median
distinct-ratio rose from **0.107 to 0.125** (~17% improvement) and
`cellsAllIdentical` dropped from **47 to 13** (~72% reduction).

The 4 over-reused, accidentally-shared closings the P11-A5 audit flagged
(samhyeong/hyeongsal, cheondeok/woldeok, bumyong/jojangung, daewoongungsil/
iljin) all dropped out of the fixtureCount=32 row. The Tier-2 closings
(통관용신/병약용신, 납음/납음오행, 지살/천의, 지살/천이궁, 조후) all dropped
out of the fixtureCount=31 row of their respective top-position
ranking (the 31-tier still has rows from other phrases that P12-A4
did not target).

## Methodology

### Discovery

Reused P11-A5's `fragment-reuse-heatmap.tsv` and `cross-fixture-report.json`.
For each top-reused phrase, scanned `data/narrative/**/*.fragments.json`
for fragments containing that phrase as a closing text token, recording
file/fragmentId/gating per occurrence
(`scan-shared-suffixes.mjs` → `shared-suffix-scan.json`).

Empirical learning across commits:

1. **Commit 1 (family base fragments)**: Modified 30 fragments with broad
   gating (e.g., `dayMasterStrength: WEAK/BALANCED`). The heatmap barely
   moved because the engine prefers more-specific gating fragments
   (currentSeason, agePhase) which still had the original closing.

2. **Commits 2-3 (overall/academic + health/movement base)**: Same
   pattern; broader-gated fragments modified, but engine-priority
   selection still picks 301 fragments with original closings.

3. **Commit 4 (high-priority 301 fragments)**: After empirically
   measuring `selectedFragments.expert.fragmentId` across all 32 sample
   fixtures, identified that **27/32 fixtures** select
   `<category>.<period>.expert.currentseason.spring_summer.301` because
   targetDate `2026-05-05` matches that gating. Modifying 48 such
   high-specificity fragments was the empirically necessary step.

## Recommendations

1. **Future phase: wildcard.001 variant authoring.** The single
   remaining `in_100pct_fixtures` row is the wildcard floor. Authoring
   2-3 variants per `*.expert.wildcard.001` fragment with selection-
   seed-based pick would push this last row off the 32-count list,
   per P11-A5 §B1 recommendation #1.

2. **Engine-side selection-seed audit.** For seed-stable cells where
   different fixtures with different selection seeds still get the same
   fragment (e.g., 27/32 picking `currentseason.spring_summer.301`),
   consider whether the selector's gating-priority rule can incorporate
   the selection-seed earlier so seeds break ties between equally-
   specific fragments.

3. **Author 2nd-tier variants per cell.** Where cells have only 1 high-
   specificity fragment matching gating (e.g., the 301 with
   currentSeason+yongshinAlignment), authoring a sibling fragment with
   the same gating but selectionSeed-tiebreak would naturally distribute
   prose across charts.
