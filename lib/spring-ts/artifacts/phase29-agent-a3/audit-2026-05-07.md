## Per-token re-review

### Paragraph #1 — `wealth.thisMonth.standard.child_teen.001` p#4

> 한 달의 작은 결산은 큰 자산이 돼요. 마지막 날 어디에 썼는지
> 한 줄로 정리하면, 다음 달의 결정이 한결 가벼워져요.

**Token: 결산은 (lexeme `결산`, "month-end accounting close")**

- Register: domain-specific personal-finance noun; not interchangeable with 정리 (which already appears in the same paragraph as the verb 정리하면), 마무리, 점검. Replacing 결산 with 정리 would create an internal repetition (정리하면 / 정리 / 정리) — a different and arguably worse readability problem than the 결-cluster.
- Semantic load: high. The whole subject of the first sentence is "the small monthly settlement is a big asset" — without 결산, the sentence loses the noun it is asserting about.
- Natural alternative: none that is non-결 *and* equivalent in register.
- **Verdict: HOLD.** The task description literally names `결산` in
  the HOLD-priority pattern (`결산`+`결정`+`한결`).

**Token: 결정이 (lexeme `결정`, "decision")**

- Register: general-Korean decision noun. Direct alternatives are 선택, 판단.
- Semantic load: medium-high. The phrase "다음 달의 결정이 한결 가벼워져요" frames future decision-confidence — the surrounding paragraph (livingTips include "큰 결정은 한 달 살피기" elsewhere) consistently uses 결정 as the household-decision noun.
- Natural alternative: "다음 달의 선택이 한결 가벼워져요" — semantically defensible but loses the "weighty / committed" connotation that 결정 carries vs. 선택 (which can mean a casual choice). The child_teen tier specifically is teaching young readers to treat money decisions seriously, so 결정 fits the pedagogical frame better than 선택.
- **Verdict: HOLD.** 선택 is a downgrade of pedagogical register, not an upgrade of clarity.

**Token: 한결 (lexeme `한결`, intensifier "even more / much")**

- Register: native-Korean comparative intensifier. The hint suggests `늘`/`일관되게` as alternatives, but these are *frequency* / *consistency* adverbs, not *comparative intensity* adverbs. `한결 가벼워져요` = "becomes much lighter [than before]"; `늘 가벼워져요` = "is always light" (frequency); `일관되게 가벼워져요` = "becomes light consistently" (manner). Both lose the comparative ("compared to without the settlement habit") force.
- Semantic load: medium. The sentence still parses without 한결 ("다음 달의 결정이 가벼워져요"), but the comparative force is what motivates the habit.
- Natural alternative: 훨씬, 한층 are themselves intensifiers in the same slot — they would shuffle a different lexeme into the position without changing readability. They are also 결-free, so the metric *would* drop, but per the task constraint "metric-gaming alone is not sufficient justification".
- **Verdict: HOLD.** The intensifier slot does carry semantic load (comparative), and 늘/일관되게 are not register-equivalent.

### Paragraph #2 — `career.today.standard.age30plus.007` p#1

> 후배·동료의 작은 질문에 짧게라도 답을 주면 자기 자리의 방향이
> 한결 단단해져요. 회의·결재로 마음이 분산될 수 있으니, 오전 한
> 시간만이라도 자기 결정의 시간으로 잡아 두면 좋아요.

(Rendered cells substitute `자기 자리의` → `자기 역할의` via post-
processor; the substitution is `결`-neutral.)

**Token: 한결 (lexeme `한결`)**

- Same lexeme as paragraph #1. Modifies `단단해져요` ("becomes firmer"). Comparative intensifier role is identical: "[much / even more] firmer than without giving the brief answers".
- Natural alternative: same 훨씬/한층 slot-shuffle problem, same 늘/일관되게 register mismatch.
- **Verdict: HOLD** for the same reason as paragraph #1.

**Token: 결재로 (lexeme `결재`, "managerial sign-off / approval")**

- Register: workplace-formal Korean. This is the office noun for the act of a manager approving a document or request. **Critically distinct from 결정**: a 결정 is the substantive choice, a 결재 is the procedural sign-off. Replacing 결재 with 결정 would collapse two distinct workplace nouns into one — exactly the same-lexical-item repetition the metric is designed to flag, *worsening* the metric, not improving it.
- Semantic load: high. The audience for this fragment is age 30-54 working adults; "회의·결재" is the canonical pair of meeting-and-sign-off distractions for this demographic. Any non-결재 alternative (e.g., "회의·서명" = "meetings·signatures") loses the workflow nuance.
- Natural alternative: 승인 ("approval") is closest, and is 결-free. "회의·승인으로 마음이 분산될 수 있으니" parses, but 승인 carries a different connotation in Korean office-Korean: 결재 implies one's own desk processing a document chain, 승인 implies receiving permission from above. The fragment is about the reader's *outgoing* desk-level processing being a distraction, so 결재 is the precise word.
- **Verdict: HOLD.** 결재's workplace register is irreplaceable here.

**Token: 결정의 (lexeme `결정`, "decision")**

- Register: same as paragraph #1's `결정이`. The phrase "자기 결정의 시간" = "time for one's own decisions" — the central advice of the paragraph (block off an hour for self-driven decision-making, separate from external sign-off requests).
- Semantic load: high. This is the noun the action verb 잡아 두면 takes as its object via 의 → 시간; without 결정, the sentence loses what kind of time to block off.
- Natural alternative: 판단의 시간 ("judgment time") is defensible but feels more abstract; 의사결정의 시간 is the formal compound but redundantly contains 결정. 선택의 시간 is a downgrade of weight, the same as in paragraph #1.
- **Verdict: HOLD.**

## Why the task's "6 정도" target is unreachable

`gyeolCluster.totalCells` counts paragraphs whose `결`-bearing token
count is `≥ 3`. Both held paragraphs sit at exactly 3 distinct tokens
each, with no double-counting headroom. Therefore:

- Paraphrase 1 token in wealth para → 0 cells from wealth para (5
  fan-out drops at once) → totalCells = 4
- Paraphrase 1 token in career para → 0 cells from career para (4
  fan-out drops at once) → totalCells = 5
- Paraphrase 1 token in each → totalCells = 0
- HOLD both → totalCells = 9

Reachable end-states: **{0, 4, 5, 9}**. **6 is not reachable** by any
single-paragraph paraphrase or pair of paraphrases on the existing
two paragraphs. The task spec offers "또는 hold confirm" precisely
because the math forbids partial motion within a single paragraph.

A future ratchet could reach 6 only by (a) refactoring the detector
to count distinct `결`-bearing lexemes (where any paragraph below 3
distinct lexemes drops out), (b) raising the threshold to ≥ 4
(currently safe — both held paragraphs sit at exactly 3), or
(c) adding new fragments outside this owned scope.
